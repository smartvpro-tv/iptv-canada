1. Check your broadband first

As a rule of thumb, plan for about 10 Mbps per HD stream and about 25 Mbps per 4K stream. An Ethernet cable or 5 GHz Wi-Fi fixes more buffering than changing provider does.

More detail: broadband speed you need, our internet speed guide and our buffering guide for the UK.

2. Check your device and app

Most services run on Firestick, Android TV, Samsung and LG smart TVs, Apple TV, PC and Mac, through apps such as IPTV Smarters Pro, TiviMate or IBO Player. Ask which apps are supported before you pay.

Device by device: devices UK viewers use.

3. Check the currency and the payment method

Look for prices in pounds and a payment method you can dispute. If a provider only takes crypto, ask why. We take Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal and Faster Payments from UK banks.

4. Check what the trial really tests

Free trials are often a few hours on an overloaded test server. A short paid trial on the real servers tells you more. Ours is £1 for 24 hours of full access on one device: IPTV trial UK.

5. Check it at peak time

Run your trial during a big evening match, not on a weekday morning. Peak-hour stability is what separates services.

6. Check the lineup and the commitment

Ask for the full channel list before you pay. Then start with one month and go longer once you trust the service.

For reference, ours: 34,000+ live channels and 195,000+ movies and series. Plans run 1, 3, 6 or 12 months for 1 to 4 devices, from £10.99 for one month on one device to £47.99 for 12 months, with no contract.

7. Check the refund policy

Read it before you pay, not after. Ours is a 7-day money-back guarantee: refund policy.

8. Check the rules

Read up on what the law says before you buy. We wrote a plain guide: Is IPTV legal in the UK?
