# How to choose an IPTV service in Canada: a practical 2026 checklist

*Written by the team behind [IPTV Canada](https://iptvv-ca.ca/). We sell a subscription, so we are not neutral. This is the checklist we would give a friend in Canada who asked which IPTV service to pick, and it works with any seller, including us.*

Last updated: November 2026

## The short version

| Check | What to look for |
|---|---|
| Internet | About 10 Mbps per HD stream, about 25 Mbps per 4K stream |
| Device and app | Your device is supported, and you know which app you will use |
| Currency and payment | Prices in CAD, Interac e-Transfer accepted |
| Trial | Runs on the live servers, not on a separate test server |
| Peak time | Stable during an evening NHL game |
| Commitment | One month first, longer only once you trust it |
| Rules | You have read what is legal where you live |

## 1. Check your internet first

As a rule of thumb, plan for about 10 Mbps per HD stream and about 25 Mbps per 4K stream. A wired connection or 5 GHz Wi-Fi fixes more buffering than changing provider does.

More detail: [internet speed you need](README.md#internet-speed-you-need) and our [buffering guide for Canada](https://iptvv-ca.ca/iptv-buffering-canada/).

## 2. Check your device and app

Most services run on Fire TV Stick, Android TV, Samsung and LG smart TVs and Apple devices, through apps such as IPTV Smarters Pro, TiviMate or IBO Player. Ask which apps are supported before you pay.

Device by device: [devices Canadians use](README.md#devices-canadians-use).

## 3. Check the currency and the payment method

Many services aimed at Canadians bill in USD. Look for prices in CAD and for Interac e-Transfer. If a provider only takes crypto, ask why.

## 4. Check what the trial really tests

Free trials often run on separate, crowded test servers. A short paid trial on the live servers tells you more. Ours is C$1.99 for 24 hours on the same servers subscribers use: [IPTV trial Canada](https://iptvv-ca.ca/iptv-free-trial-canada/).

## 5. Check it at peak time

Run your trial during an evening NHL game, not on a weekday morning. Peak-hour stability is what separates services, and peak-hour buffering is the number one reason people switch.

## 6. Check the lineup and the commitment

Ask for the full channel list, in English and in French, before you pay. Then start with one month and go longer once you trust the service.

For reference, ours: 34,000+ live channels and 195,000+ movies and series, with Canadian, US and French-language channels. Plans run 1, 3, 6 or 12 months for 1 to 4 devices, from C$20.99 for one month on one device up to C$66.99 for four devices, with no contract.

## 7. Check the rules where you live

Read up on what is legal before you buy. We wrote a plain guide: [Is IPTV legal in Canada?](https://iptvv-ca.ca/is-iptv-legal-in-canada/)
